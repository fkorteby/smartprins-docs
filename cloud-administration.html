Smart Restaurant Administration Last updated: July 31, 2026

Cloud Administration Guide

The Smart Restaurant Administration panel is your central hub for managing every aspect of your restaurant operations from the cloud.

Overview

It is composed of the following sections accessible from the left sidebar: Dashboard, Kitchen, Admin Chat, Client, Online, Marketing, POS, Menu, Inventory, Customization, and Reporting.

Logging In

Open the Smart Kiosk Administration login page. The screen displays the title "Welcome back!" with a subtitle prompting you to enter your username address and password to access the admin panel.

Fill in the two fields as follows:

  • Email Address - enter your assigned username or email
  • Password - enter your secure password

You can tick Remember me to stay logged in on this device. Complete the reCAPTCHA by checking the "I'm not a robot" box, then click the blue Log In button.

Upon successful login, you will land on the Smart Restaurant Administration dashboard.

Logging Out

To log out, locate your profile in the upper-left corner of the sidebar showing your name and the CLIENT MANAGER label. Click the small dropdown arrow next to your name. A menu appears with three options: Edit Account, Support, and Logout. Click Logout to end your session.

Next Step: Dashboard

3. Dashboard

The Dashboard is the first screen you see after logging in. It gives you a real-time overview of your business performance.

The top bar on the right contains three filter buttons: Select Branch, Select Kiosk, and Select Source, which let you narrow down the data shown across the entire dashboard.

The main content area contains the following:

Area Description
KPI CardsFour cards display Today Revenue, Total Revenue, Today Orders, and Total Orders. Each card shows the current value along with a percentage trend indicator.
Overview PanelShows Total Installed Kiosks, Average Revenue Per Kiosk in SAR, and Average Order Time in HH:MM:SS format.
Revenue ChartA line chart showing revenue over time with 7D, 15D, 1M, and 3M filters.
Revenue By MonthA secondary chart showing monthly revenue trends with 6M, 1Y, and 2Y filters.

Scrolling down reveals additional charts including Revenue By Products shown as a pie chart, an Orders bar chart, Revenue By Branch, and an Orders Durations graph. A detailed orders table on the right lists recent transactions with their branch, duration, total, and a view button.

4. Kitchen

The Kitchen section connects to the Smart Kitchen App, which displays live orders on kitchen display screens. The kitchen screen organises orders into four tabs at the top: Placed, Active, Hold, and History, each showing a count badge.

Each order card shows the order number, items with their selected options in both English and Arabic, and four action buttons at the bottom: Queued, Hold, Print, and Abort. The card also displays the Order at time, Pickup date, Status, and a Delayed By timer. A Select Table dropdown appears in the top right and a live clock is shown in the top-right corner.

For a full dedicated guide to the Kitchen App, refer to the Kitchen App User Manual.

6. Online

6.1 Drivers

Navigate to Online -> Drivers to manage your delivery drivers.

To add a new driver, click + Add Driver. The Add Driver form contains the following fields:

FieldDetails
Name *Driver's full name (e.g., Driver 1)
Client *Select the associated company from the dropdown
Phone *Driver's contact number
Status *Toggle to enable or disable the driver
BranchesSelect the branch or branches this driver serves
DescriptionOptional notes about the driver

Click Submit to save, or Close to cancel.

6.2 Online Orders

Navigate to Online -> Online Orders to monitor all orders placed through online channels.

A Total summary bar at the top shows Number of Orders, Subtotal in SAR, VAT in SAR, and Total in SAR. Below the summary, four filter dropdowns let you narrow results by Branch, Driver, Customer, and Order Status.

The orders table shows the following columns: Id, Branch, Date Order, Pickup Date, Date Dispatch, Table, Total, Driver, Status, and Actions.

6.3 B2B Orders

Navigate to Online -> B2B Orders to monitor business-to-business transactions.

The page follows the same layout as Online Orders, with a Total summary bar at the top and the same four filter dropdowns. The table columns are: Id, Branch, Customer, Ticket, Date Order, Duration, Takeout, Subtotal, VAT, Total, and Status.

8. Customization

Navigate to Customization in the left sidebar. The Customization section contains four subsections: Products, Options, Product Option, and Sort Menu.

8.1 Products

Navigate to Customization -> Products. This page is titled Products Customization and shows a table with columns for Id, Branch, Product, Price, Status, and Actions.

To add a customization, click + Add Customization. The Add Customization form contains Branch, Product, Price, and Status.

8.2 Product Option

Navigate to Customization -> Product Option. This page shows a table with columns for Id, Branch, Product, Option, Price, ExtraPrice, Status, and Actions.

8.3 Sort Menu

Navigate to Customization -> Sort Menu. The Sort Menu screen has three tabs at the top: category/product, family/option, and Offers/Groups.

The category/product tab shows a left panel with your Categories list and a right panel with the products within the selected category. Use the up and down arrow icons on each row to reorder items. Changes are reflected immediately across kiosks and menus.

The Sort Menu feature requires a Pro or Advanced subscription.

10. Reporting

Navigate to Reporting in the left sidebar. The Reporting section contains four subsections: Orders, admin_zatca_orders, Inventory Sales, and Charts.

10.1 Orders

A Total summary bar at the top shows Number of Orders, Subtotal in SAR, VAT in SAR, and Total in SAR. Four filter dropdowns let you refine the results: Select Branch, Select Kiosk, Select Status, and Select Payment Method. A date range label shows the active period, and an Export button allows downloading the filtered data.

The orders table contains the following columns: Id, Branch, Kiosk, Ticket, Date Order, Takeout, Subtotal, Vat, Total, Payment Type, and Status.

10.2 Product Sales

This page shows a Total summary bar at the top with Number of Products, Number of Branches, Total Qty Sold, and Total Sales in SAR. Three filter dropdowns are available: Select Branch, Select Category, and Select Product. A date range picker and an Export button appear on the right.

The table shows the following columns: Id, Product, Ref, Category, Branch, Qty Sold, Unit Price, and Total.

10.3 Charts

The Charts page allows you to generate interactive visual reports for a selected date range. At the top of the page, set your date range using the Choose A Date Range field. Three filter dropdowns sit in the top right: Select Branch, Select Kiosk, and Select Source. Once configured, click the green Generate button to render the selected chart.

Available chart types include line charts, pie charts, and bar charts. After selecting a chart type and clicking Generate, the page loads an interactive chart with the title, date range, and company name in the header. A Back button returns you to the chart selection screen.
