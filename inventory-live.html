Step 05 — Going Live Your First Inventory Count and What Happens Next

Your menu is defined, ingredients are configured, suppliers are registered, and recipes are linked. You are now transitioning from setup mode to live operations. From this point forward, the system will automatically track every sale, purchase, and stock movement in real time.

Overview

Before going live, you need to establish your starting inventory balances. This is done through your first inventory count — a physical walkthrough of your storage areas where you record the actual on-hand quantity of every ingredient. Once submitted, this count sets the baseline from which all future movements are tracked.

Navigate to Inventory → Stock → Inventory Counts, then click + Add to open the count form. Add each physically counted ingredient as a line with its actual measured quantity, then click Submit.

Tip: Your first count is the most important one. Take the time to count accurately — every future variance report will be measured against this starting point.

What Happens Automatically

Once your first count is submitted, the system takes over the heavy lifting:

  • Real-Time Stock Deductions: Every POS sale reads the recipe and instantly deducts the exact ingredient quantities consumed.
  • Live Stock Status: On-hand quantities update continuously across all branches. No end-of-day syncing is required.
  • Audit Trails: Every movement (sales, waste, transfers, purchases) leaves a timestamped record.

The Daily Inventory Rhythm

A typical operational day moves through the following sequence. Not every step happens every day at every branch — but understanding the full rhythm helps your team know where each action fits.

  • Start of day: Perform a stock count if scheduled. Verify stock levels before production begins.
  • During the day: Receive purchase deliveries and record purchase receptions. Log waste as it occurs. Initiate transfers if a branch needs stock from another location.
  • Sales run continuously: The POS consumes stock automatically with every transaction. No action required from your team.
  • End of day: Log any remaining waste. Perform an end-of-day stock count if your operation requires it. Review stock status for items running low before the next day.

What Your Team Manages Daily

While consumption is automatic, your team is responsible for logging everything else that impacts stock:

  • Receiving Purchases: Log incoming deliveries via Inventory → Purchasing → Purchase Receptions to increase stock levels and record supplier costs.
  • Logging Waste: Discarded, spoiled, or incorrectly prepped items must be recorded via Inventory → Stock → Wastes so they aren't incorrectly calculated as "consumed by sales."
  • Ongoing Inventory Counts: Perform regular physical counts (Inventory → Stock → Inventory Counts) to catch and correct discrepancies.
  • Managing Transfers: If moving stock between locations, initiate a Stock Transfer at the sending branch and a Stock Reception at the receiving branch via Inventory → Transfer.
